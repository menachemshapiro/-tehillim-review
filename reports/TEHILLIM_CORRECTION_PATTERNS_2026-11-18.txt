PURPOSE AND LIMITATIONS

The user asked for ALL examples of recurring correction/chip patterns, and ALL decisions, updated with the new cumulative JSON export. The source captures ID, choice, detail, note, corrected_text, updated_at; it does NOT embed the original scan crop or the losing candidates. Therefore claims about physical chip shapes or causes are hypotheses until matched against the original images. A count:0 confirms the chosen zero count, not a mechanism. Whole-word choices reflect the user's preference, not automatically an objective cross-edition truth. Record notes are verbatim with their line breaks represented as literal \n.
The new export is a cumulative snapshot and must replace, not be blindly appended to, the original dataset. A previously reviewed decision may be revised; the latest version of that decision is authoritative for this report.
