PURPOSE AND LIMITS

This report classifies recurring print/dot/niqqud/taamim recognition symptoms from saved review decisions. It does NOT directly inspect the original scan crops, which are not present in the export. These categories therefore describe repeated CORRECTION PATTERNS, not proven physical shapes of chipped or broken type. Choices such as count:1 need not mean the reconstruction was incorrect. An empty corrected_text field is normal for a mark-count review; it is not evidence of a missing word. Specific words are printed exactly as saved, and notes are preserved verbatim (newlines escaped).

GENERAL NUMERICAL OVERVIEW

The strongest recurring candidate rules to test against scans are: (a) false meteg, (b) cholam vs revia, (c) single qamats vs patach+tsere, (d) broken/merged vowel dots, (e) munach mistaken as extra sign or dot, (f) ink artifacts.
