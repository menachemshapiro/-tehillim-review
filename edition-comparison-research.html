Purpose and scope

This comparison asks whether the new edition is intended to be a faithful recreation of the old edition, or merely a lookalike based on a completely different textual source. It does not attempt to determine which edition is more accurate or whether any particular change was intentional.

The comparison focuses on two categories of differences: changes in vocalization (nikud) and changes in cantillation (ta’amim). Both demonstrate differences in the underlying text, rather than merely in its visual presentation.

3. Conclusion

The addition and omission of dageshim in the bet, together with the differing vowel configuration at Psalm 32:6, provide evidence of changes beyond the graphical appearance of the printed text. The cantillation comparisons further demonstrate differences, although some recurring changes may reflect alternative conventions for rendering the same sign. The conclusion does not depend on establishing which edition is correct, or on attributing the changes to any particular editorial process.
